## Design Context
### Users
AI engineering internship candidates and the hiring reviewer. Developers understand code but do not know this fictional workstation. The first screen must explain that the product itself is broken and identify the bounded repair entry.
### Use case
Implement an AI agent that restores the workstation’s own document, search and export functions, then continuously detects and repairs new faults. No audio. The maintenance shell must remain visible during failure.
### Personality
Precise, legible, restrained. Use the supplied 万象硅芯 logo.
### Direction
A light recovery console: real failed capabilities and real self-check evidence. Warm neutral surfaces, navy type, restrained blue actions; red only for actual failures, green only after independent checks. Do not show fake boot percentages or fake AI reasoning.
